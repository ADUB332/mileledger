Location permissions

MileLedger uses location only while a trip is actively being tracked. Background location is used only during active trips, including when your phone is locked, so the app can calculate mileage and save the driven route.

Exporting mileage logs

Use the export option in the app to create a mileage log for your visible or filtered trips. If you apply date filters, the export will include the trips currently shown in the app.

Deleting local data

You can delete local trip data from inside the app. This removes trip history, route points, reimbursement settings, and manual entries stored on your device.

Troubleshooting

  • Make sure location permissions are enabled for MileLedger.
  • Allow background location if you want trips to continue tracking while your phone is locked.
  • Keep the app open or active when starting a trip so tracking begins correctly.
  • If mileage looks incorrect, check whether location permission or GPS signal was interrupted.
