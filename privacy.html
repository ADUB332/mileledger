MileLedger Privacy Policy

Effective date: UPDATE_EFFECTIVE_DATE

MileLedger is a local-first GPS mileage tracker for work trips, reimbursement logs, and clean exports.

Information stored by the app

MileLedger may store trip history, route points, reimbursement settings, manual entries, exported backup data, and app preferences locally on your device.

Location data

MileLedger uses location only while a trip is actively being tracked. Background location is used only during active trips, including when your phone is locked, so the app can calculate mileage and save the driven route.

Saved route points are used to display the trip map in Trip History.

Local-only storage

Trip history and route data are stored locally on your device. MileLedger does not upload trip history or route data to a server in the current version.

Cloud sync

MileLedger does not use cloud sync in the current version.

Ads and tracking

MileLedger does not use ads and does not sell user data.

Exports and sharing

If you export or share a mileage log or backup file, you control where that file goes. MileLedger is not responsible for third-party apps, services, or people you choose to share files with.

Deleting data

You can delete local data from inside the app. This removes trip history, route points, reimbursement settings, manual entries, and app preferences stored on your device.
